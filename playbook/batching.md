# Batching and red lines

## Batch plan

**Batch 1 (1–2 h): all `P0`.** The point is certainty: these are proven free, badge-free, self-serve. You end with 10+ real submission records and a working rhythm.

**Batch 2 (2–3 h): `P1` that need an account.** Sign in once (most accept Google OAuth), then go site to site without logging in again.

**Batch 3 (as needed): `P2`.** Accept the reality — nofollow, human review, "needs customer reviews / repo stars first". Value is brand-name surface area, not ranking.

**`P3`: pull it out and ask.** Reciprocal link in your footer, paid acceleration, Top-3-only dofollow. That is a product/SEO decision, not an execution detail. **Default: no.**

**`X`: never.** Wrong category, hard gate, dead entry, not a directory. Scan the list once and save the most expensive thing you have.

## Red lines — hit any one, do not submit

1. The free tier requires a badge or a followed backlink on your own site.
2. The site only accepts a category your product is not in (AI-only, Android-only, productivity-only).
3. The submission requires a factual declaration that is not true (classic: "no AI-written code").
4. The only account path is email+password on a site you cannot complete verification for, with no OAuth option.
5. Submission is paid-only, with no free queue.
6. The site is a list-of-lists, a paid submission agency, or a project marketplace — not a directory.
7. Opening it gives 404 / cert error / parked domain / infinite Cloudflare loop / a redirect to a casino.

**`$0` is not free.** Free plans frequently require the badge to stay installed permanently, checked periodically. The headline says free; the second screen of the pricing page says "install our badge". Always read to the bottom of the pricing page.

## Judgement calls worth writing down

- A badge offered *by the platform on its own profile* (e.g. "launch winner") is not the same as a badge you must install on your site. Only the second one violates the no-badge rule.
- A free nofollow listing still has value (referral traffic, brand search, a page that ranks for your name). Decide per site whether that is worth 10 minutes — do not pretend it is a dofollow link.
- "Approved" does not always mean "live": some sites approve into a future launch window weeks away. Record the window, not "live".
- Queue length changes the value calculation: a 3–4 month free queue with nofollow is a `P2`, whatever the DR.

## Pacing

- 1–2 batches per week. Submitting 30 sites in a day means waiting 4 months once instead of having links land continuously.
- Weekly: check the inbox for verification mail and approval notices.
- Monthly: check queued items for publication; spot-check 10 `P0`/`P1` entries to see whether the submission page still exists.
